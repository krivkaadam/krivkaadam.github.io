A little about me

ADAM KŘIVKA

I’m a Computer Science student at FIT, Brno University of Technology, and this website is slowly becoming a collection of things I’ve built along the way. Most of what you’ll find here is a work in progress: small tools, experiments, half-finished ideas, and the occasional project that actually made it all the way to the finish line.

I like building things that solve real problems in my life, even when those problems are very specific to me. One of my goals is to create a personal portfolio of apps and programs that I genuinely find useful in my everyday life: all in one place. They don’t necessarily have to make sense to anyone else. If something makes my life a little easier, or gives me a reason to finally implement something I’ve only encountered in theory, that’s already a good enough reason to build it.

... and so on.

"Not just about computers."

Outside of IT, I spend a lot of time playing badminton and beach volleyball, skiing, running, and, whenever I get the chance, SURFING. I love good food, travelling, mountains, praias, and generally finding excuses to be somewhere new. I am also involved in volunteering with international students and working as an ESN Causes Manager, which has given me the chance to meet people from all over the world and be part of things that have nothing to do with programming.

One place that has especially stuck with me is Portugal, where I had the opportunity to do my Erasmus at FCUP in Porto. Living abroad, travelling, meeting people from completely different backgrounds, learning português, and discovering new places have probably taught me just as much as university has.

So this portfolio isn’t really meant to be a perfect showcase of polished software. It’s more like a record of curiosity - a place where I can keep the things I build, experiment with ideas, learn by actually making them, and occasionally turn a completely unnecessary idea into something surprisingly useful.

Despite all of that, I would be really glad if at least someone liked even a tiny bit of my work haha. Explore!

Praia do Magoito Erasmus @ FCUP · Surf & Sun
